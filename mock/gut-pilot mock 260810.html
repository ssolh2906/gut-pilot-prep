Rarefaction — set the depth threshold

Every sample below the line is excluded from diversity calculations. This is the platform's core human-in-the-loop gate.

Reviewer proposal

I picked 4,200 reads/sample because rarefaction curves plateau for 22 of 24 samples by that depth, and Schloss (2024) found rarefaction — repeated subsampling, not single-pass rarefying — gives the highest statistical power for alpha/beta diversity when depth is uneven across groups. Two samples (H-09, C-04) stay below the line and are excluded rather than force the whole cohort down to their depth.

Why not go deeper, like 6,000?
At 6,000 reads I'd also have to drop S-15, S-19 and C-11, C-14 — five more samples, which weakens the Healthy vs CRC contrast. 4,200 keeps 22/24, and curves have already plateaued for 92% of samples by then. See Schloss 2024 → on why deeper isn't automatically better once curves flatten.

Reviewer flag — check your expectation, not just the p-value

A common prior is “CRC gut = lower diversity.” This cohort shows the opposite lean: richness (Observed / Chao1) trends slightly higher in CRC, and Shannon is flat (p=0.62). That is consistent with the literature — CRC dysbiosis here is taxon-specific enrichment (oral-origin taxa), not a global loss of diversity. Don’t over-read the alpha panel; the real signal is in differential abundance below.
